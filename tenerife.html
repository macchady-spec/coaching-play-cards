FIBA’s March 2024 Tenerife–Jerusalem breakdown describes Bruno Fitipaldo attacking a closeout with one dribble. The defender recovering to the corner stepped back toward the drive. These courts apply that timing to our corner catch, with shooter 1 above the break. The X2 help and stay decisions extend the baseline drive as a drawn coaching example. Read FIBA’s breakdown.
