5 Twice

Two chances for your shooting big. One empty corner for your guard.

1 Guard · 2 Two-screen setter · 3 Right-hand slasher · 4 Corner shooter · 5 Shooting big. Squares are defenders.
Timeout call

“5 Twice. Right corner empty. Two, pin for five. Five, shoot it or hand it back to one. Two, screen five’s new defender. One, rim or throwback. Three, hold left and attack right if yours helps.”

Teach the timing

First window: 5 brushes 2’s planted screen, ready to shoot beyond the right-slot arc. If denied, step toward 1 for a safe catch. If there’s no catch, 1 keeps it; there is no handoff.

Second window: 1 follows the pass over 5’s outside shoulder. As 1 takes the ball, 2 sets again in the recovery path of whoever is guarding 5. After 1 clears, 5 flares back to the top. Both screens need a legal stationary base, time and space.

Third player: 3 holds the left wing during 1’s drive. If X3 only stunts, 1 keeps the dribble. On a real commitment, pass before getting trapped below the rim. Once 1 clears, 3 drives right if the middle is available; if X3 takes away that hand, swing it instead.
