# Coaching play cards

**[Open the shareable coaching playbook](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/coach.html)** for switch attacks on screens, drives and handoffs. Play the 1–5 seal or guard attack and use the defensive boards to discuss getting back on the shot, forcing left on a long closeout, and containing a right drive that spins back left. No account required.

[Attacking the switch](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/switches.html#screen/hunt/0) opens four animated teaching boards: Cluj flare, Corner lift, 5 Twice and Attack the X-out. Choose the defense, play to a decision, and share a link to that beat. The courts show authored coaching examples. Each play includes its sources where applicable.

The original [5 Twice](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/) and [False Exchange](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/false-exchange.html) boards are also available.

[Quick pass or one dribble?](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/tenerife.html#help/catch) compares two reads from the same corner catch. Both courts pause on X3’s reaction; switch the response to play the pass or the baseline drive. [Continue the baseline drive](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/tenerife.html#x2/drive) to see X2 step across or stay with 4, then play the drop pass and the near-side finish together.
