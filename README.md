# Coaching play cards

[Attacking the switch](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/switches.html#screen/hunt/0) opens four animated teaching boards: Cluj flare, Corner lift, 5 Twice and Attack the X-out. Choose the defense, play to a decision, and share a link to that beat. The courts show authored coaching examples. Each play includes its sources where applicable.

The original [5 Twice](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/) and [False Exchange](https://macchady-spec.github.io/coaching-play-cards/false-exchange.html) boards are also available.
